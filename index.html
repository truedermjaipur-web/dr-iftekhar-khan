Meet Dr. Iftekhar Khan

Expertise backed by experience.
Care built around people.

Dr. Iftekhar Khan is a dermatologist and aesthetic physician practising at TrueDerm in Jaipur, with a clinical background from AIIMS Delhi. His practice is built on a simple principle: every patient's skin, hair and concerns are different, and treatment should be planned accordingly rather than applied as a one-size-fits-all protocol.

From medical dermatology to advanced aesthetic procedures, Dr. Khan focuses on accurate diagnosis first, followed by treatment options that are explained clearly so patients can make informed decisions about their own care.

Frequently asked questions

Good to know before your visit

How can I book an appointment?
You can book an appointment by calling the clinic, messaging on WhatsApp, or using the "Book an Appointment" button on this website.
What should I bring to my consultation?
Please bring any previous prescriptions, test reports or treatment records relevant to your concern, along with a valid ID.
How long does a consultation take?
Consultation length varies by concern, but most first visits take between 20 and 40 minutes to allow for a thorough assessment.
Do I need a prior appointment?
Appointments are recommended to avoid waiting time, though walk-ins may be accommodated depending on availability.
What treatments and services are available?
TrueDerm offers medical and aesthetic dermatology, including mole removal, collagen-focused treatments, Botox, bridal skincare, PRP and hair transplant, and vitiligo surgery. See the Treatments section above for details.
Where is the clinic located?
TrueDerm is located at Bhagadiya Bhawan, J-26 (First Floor), Subhash Marg, C-Scheme, Ashok Nagar, Jaipur – 302001. Full directions are available in the Contact section below.
Are follow-up consultations available?
Yes, follow-up visits are an important part of most treatment plans and can be scheduled as advised by Dr. Khan.
