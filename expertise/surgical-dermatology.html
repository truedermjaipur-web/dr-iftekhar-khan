Areas of expertise

Surgical Dermatology

Mole and skin lesion removal, and vitiligo surgery, performed with careful pre- and post-care.

What this covers

Surgical dermatology handles the removal of moles and skin lesions of cosmetic or medical concern, along with surgical options for stable vitiligo. Every case starts with a clinical assessment to confirm the procedure is appropriate before anything is scheduled.

The procedure

Minor procedures are carried out at the clinic under local anaesthesia, following sterile protocol. You'll be given clear instructions beforehand — what to expect during the procedure, how long it typically takes, and how to prepare.

Recovery and follow-up

Aftercare instructions cover wound care, activity restrictions where relevant, and signs to watch for during healing. A structured follow-up is scheduled to check healing progress and, where applicable, review any lab results from a removed lesion.
